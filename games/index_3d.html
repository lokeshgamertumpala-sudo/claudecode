🎯 Aim & Fire: Move cursor or touch to aim. The cannon fires high-powered projectiles automatically. Hold to charge shots!
🟢 Gate Multipliers: Shoot gates to crack them open! Green (+) and Blue (×) multiply your crowd. Avoid Red (-) and Orange (÷)!
