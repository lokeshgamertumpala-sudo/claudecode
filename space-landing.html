Exploration Timeline

Key milestones shaping the next decade of space exploration

2024

Artemis II Crewed Flyby

First crewed mission around the Moon since Apollo 17, testing Orion systems with astronauts aboard.

2024

Europa Clipper Launch

NASA's flagship mission to Jupiter's ocean moon begins its 5.5-year journey to assess Europa's habitability.

2025

VIPER Moon Landing

First resource mapping rover touches down at lunar South Pole to prospect for water ice deposits.

2026

Artemis III Moon Landing

First woman and next man walk on the lunar surface, establishing sustainable presence at the South Pole.

2027

Roman Telescope Launch

Wide-field infrared observatory begins survey of dark energy, exoplanets, and galactic structure.

2028

Mars Sample Return Launch

Earth Return Orbiter and Sample Retrieval Lander depart for Mars to collect cached samples.

2028

Dragonfly Launch to Titan

Rotorcraft begins 7-year journey to Saturn's largest moon to explore prebiotic chemistry.

2030s

Lunar Base Operational

Permanent human presence established at lunar South Pole with in-situ resource utilization.

2033

Mars Samples Return to Earth

First pristine Martian samples arrive on Earth for laboratory analysis worldwide.

2034

Dragonfly Arrives at Titan

Rotorcraft begins aerial exploration of Titan's diverse environments over 2.7-year mission.
